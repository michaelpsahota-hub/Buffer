Pop quiz: can a psychologist prescribe medication in Australia?

No. Psychologists can assess and diagnose many mental health conditions, but they're not medical doctors, so they can't prescribe. Not even clinical psychologists.
With a Mental Health Care Plan, Medicare covers part of the cost of up to 10 individual sessions a year.

Title: Anyone can call themselves a counsellor. What to look for · 57 characters
Alternatives: "Is your counsellor registered? How to tell" · "Counsellor and therapist aren't protected titles"

In most of Australia, anyone can call themselves a counsellor or a therapist. These aren't protected titles.

That doesn't mean counsellors aren't good. Many are excellent. It just means it's worth checking.
Look for registration with a professional body, like the Australian Counselling Association (ACA) or PACFA, the Psychotherapy and Counselling Federation of Australia. Their registered members meet training standards, have regular supervision and keep learning every year.
Counselling generally isn't covered by Medicare, so ask about the cost upfront.
